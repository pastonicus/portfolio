I’m a design leader who works at the point where decisions get difficult: AI systems, workflows, teams, and product strategy. I read the shapes of a problem, test the constraints, and turn complexity into a set of useful, human options.

My work is grounded in research, service design, accessible production systems, and the ethics of intelligent software. I care less about spectacle and more about whether people can understand, trust, and act on a better decision.

That has shaped projects across pharma, logistics, fintech, education, and public-interest design. The outcome is usually the same: a more useful interface, a more durable team practice, and a path from confusion to momentum.
